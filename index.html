The Job

Steal what isn't yours

Flunklug is a multiplayer co-op horror game about stealing what isn't yours. Break into facilities, caverns and worse with your friends, grab everything of value you can carry, and get out before the things down there catch you first.

The whole world is physics-driven. Everything reacts, breaks and gets in the way.

Some gadgets exist purely to open up parts of the map you shouldn't be in. That's where the rarer, more valuable stuff is kept.

The Loop

Every escape pays for the next

Get in, get loaded up, get out alive. Then spend the haul on whatever gets you deeper next time.

01

Break in

Facilities, caverns and worse. Getting through the front door is your problem to solve.

02

Grab everything

If it has value and you can carry it, it's coming with you. Your hands and bag are the only inventory.

03

Get out

The things down there don't wait for you to loot. Don't get caught.

04

Sell & gear up

Cash in the scrap, unlock new gadgets and weapons, and push deeper than the run before.
