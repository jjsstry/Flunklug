About the game

What Flunklug is, what you'll be doing in it, and the answers to the questions people keep asking in the Discord.

Flunklug is a multiplayer co-op horror game about stealing what isn't yours. Break into facilities, caverns and worse with your friends, grab everything of value you can carry, and get out before the things down there catch you first.

Everything is physical

The world is fully physics-driven. Objects react, break and get in the way - and so do you. There is no menu inventory to hide behind: if you want to take something out with you, you have to actually carry it, up ladders, along beams and past whatever is following the noise you just made.

Every escape pays for the next

Scrap is only worth something once it's above ground. Haul it out, sell it, and spend the money on gadgets and weapons that make the next run survivable. Certain gadgets exist to break or blast open parts of the map you have no business being in - which is, naturally, where the rarer and more valuable things are kept.

Going in as a group

Up to fifteen players can be in it together, with co-op, PvP and PvE all supported. Splitting up covers more ground and finds more loot. It also means nobody is watching your back when the lights find something that shouldn't be standing there.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

When does Flunklug come out?

Flunklug is coming in August 2026. Wishlisting on the Meta Quest store is the fastest way to be told the moment it goes live, and the Discord gets the news first.

Which headsets does Flunklug support?

Meta Quest 2, Meta Quest 3, Meta Quest 3S and Meta Quest Pro. It runs standalone on the headset, so no PC is needed, but an internet connection is required.

How many people can play Flunklug together?

Up to 15 players. The game supports co-op, PvP and PvE play, and it is built around going in as a group.

Is Flunklug free?

Flunklug is free. The Meta Quest store listing notes that the game also includes in-game purchases.

Is Flunklug scary?

It is a horror game, and it is rated PEGI 12 with a horror content warning. The pressure comes from being hunted while your hands are full rather than from graphic content.

Is Flunklug coming to Steam or PC VR?

Meta Quest is the confirmed platform, and Flunklug may come to Steam in the future. Anything new gets announced on this site, on the Discord and on the TikTok.

Who is making Flunklug?

Screen VR Studios, an independent VR studio based in England. They also made Fear Massive and Marmo, both available now on Meta Quest.
